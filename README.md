# FloydbaAchievements
A small mod for **Casualties: Unknown**, showcasing the implementation of [**CUAchievements**](https://github.com/J1ND3R9/CUAchievements/)!

## List of achievements
### Instants
- Where is that damn battery? | Complete the last layer.
- I will definitely survive | Complete the last layer while unchipped.
- The only one stronger than me is... | Raise your Strength to level 25. Maximum xp gain mult is 2.
- Patience and hard work... | Raise your Resilience to level 25. Maximum xp gain mult is 2.
- And they said only Milkys were smart | Raise your Intelligence to level 25. Maximum xp gain mult is 2.
- Alpha Ray Disco | Get a lethal dose of radiation sickness.
- What I was taught! | Kill the creature by throwing a rake at it.
- Alan, We are SO fucked... | Meet Elder Thornback.
- Lobotomy | Fall headfirst onto a spike trap.
- Dead God | Unlock all achievements for this mod!

### Counters
- And what do we have in our little pockets? | Craft 100 unique items
- Paint It, Yellow | Lose 15 liters of blood in a single run.
- Monster Hunter | Kill three Elder Thornback in a single run.
